Fresh from the Sea

A Taste of the Italian Coast

Born from the rugged beauty of the Italian coastline, Scoglio brings the flavours of the sea to the heart of Rome. Every plate begins with the freshest catch of the day.

Handmade pasta, simple ingredients and time-honoured recipes, served with warmth in a setting that feels like a seaside trattoria.

Made Fresh, Every Day

We work with trusted local producers to bring you seasonal seafood, hand-rolled pasta and classic Italian desserts. Come hungry and stay as long as you like.
